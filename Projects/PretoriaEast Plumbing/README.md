# Pretoria East Plumbing Website Wireframing

## 📌 Purpose
This README outlines the wireframing plan for Pretoria East Plumbing’s new website.  
The goal is to establish a clear visual structure before moving into design and development.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Define the layout and navigation flow.
- Ensure user-friendly access to services and contact information.
- Highlight trust-building elements (testimonials, certifications).
- Optimize for mobile-first design.

---

## 🗂️ Wireframe Structure

### 1. Homepage
- **Header:** Logo, navigation menu (Home, Services, About, Contact).
- **Hero Section:** Large banner with emergency call-to-action (phone number).
- **Quick Links:** Buttons for “Book a Service” and “Emergency Plumbing.”
- **Featured Services:** Icons + short descriptions.
- **Testimonials Preview:** 2–3 customer quotes.
- **Footer:** Contact info, social media links, business hours.

---

### 2. Services Page
- **Intro Section:** Short overview of plumbing expertise.
- **Service Categories:**
  - Repairs (leaks, burst pipes)
  - Installations (geysers, bathrooms, kitchens)
  - Maintenance (drain cleaning, inspections)
- **Call-to-Action:** “Request a Quote” button.

---

### 3. About Page
- **Company Story:** Brief history and mission.
- **Team Section:** Photos + short bios of plumbers.
- **Certifications & Licenses:** Icons or badges.

---

### 4. Contact Page
- **Contact Form:** Name, email, phone, message.
- **Emergency Hotline:** Prominent phone number.
- **Map Integration:** Pretoria East location.
- **Business Hours:** Clear availability.

---

## 📈 Wireframing Guidelines
- Use **low-fidelity wireframes** first (boxes, placeholders).
- Focus on **layout and hierarchy**, not colors or fonts.
- Ensure **mobile-first responsiveness**.
- Keep navigation simple and intuitive.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Suggested
- **Figma** (preferred for collaborative wireframing).
- Alternatives: Adobe XD, Sketch, or Balsamiq.

---

## 📌 Next Steps
1. Create low-fidelity wireframes for all pages.
2. Review with Pretoria East Plumbing stakeholders.
3. Iterate based on feedback.
4. Move to high-fidelity mockups with branding and visuals.
